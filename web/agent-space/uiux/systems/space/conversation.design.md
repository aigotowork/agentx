# Conversation Block contract

Conversation keeps exact identity, two record channels (AgentX messages and Provider Transcript), history, context, and a composer for messages reachable together. Product adapters own native reads, permissions, and operations. Empty, unavailable, failed, and pending records remain distinct. Appearance never changes capabilities; local design scenes can edit drafts without creating records or receipts.

Folio and Signal preserve this contract while changing only reader surfaces. Their `conversation-trim` asset role decorates the history-to-composer join: Folio repeats its 160×12 page edge horizontally; Signal places one 160×12 panel edge. Both are decorative and retain the semantic border as fallback. The canonical SVGs remain authored under `src/scene/art/studio/refined/`; this UIUX system owns only their presentation binding.
