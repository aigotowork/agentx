# Lunar · Farside office

A lunar operations office for the existing Space room and cast. The observation ports establish the location, a quiet deck leaves a clear path between stations, and a bright airlock marks the way in. Cold light enters from the back left. Desaturated cobalt falls away toward the lower right. Safety orange marks equipment that can be approached or operated, and ice white leads the eye to faces and contact edges.

The Maker is a squared repair rig with a clamp shoulder and heavy paired boots. The Designer has a dish, slender legs and lime optics. The Keeper is a broad tracked vault with a white scanner. The Scout has a round dome, rangefinder and sprung landing legs. Their shapes remain distinct with the glyphs removed. These costumes belong to the artwork and do not encode agent state. Each figure uses the existing bottom-center contact point, and works as the directory portrait.

The shell is a cutaway of a pressurized field station. Repeated wall and floor masters use the shared Studio geometry. Observation windows carry the horizon and secondary light, mesh flooring stays low contrast, and the lounge uses darker hex mesh to leave clear visual room for the cast. A cat-shaped courier drone at the mailbox carries the human endpoint cue.

The shared [Studio art contract](../../AGENTS.md) owns dimensions and anchors. All pictured hardware and cast members are original vector masters in this directory. The glyph silhouettes preserve the shared work tool, speech bubble, attention pennant and foot-level selection meanings.
