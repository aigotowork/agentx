# Newsprint · 漫画社

A late shift in a two-ink comics print room. The back wall is the darkest panel: cobalt night windows, a pale moon edged with red, and a proof pinned between the frames. Warm paper across the cutaway floor gives faces, boots and status badges room to read. The workbench area echoes a press bed, the lounge gets a large halftone carpet, and the human entrance puts a cat beside the post box. Every piece starts here as an original editable vector.

Paper `#f7e7bf`, ink `#211a26`, vermilion `#d9463d`, and cobalt `#315696` set the printing palette. Dark cast shadows fall below and to the right. Red slips out from under the window frame and moon, while blue shadows and sparse dot screens tell fore from aft. The ground uses a lighter screen than the props and back wall. The shell colors for comparison are courtyard `#ead5ae`, trim `#211a26`, edge `#d9463d`, and shadow `#31569638`.

At 64 × 72 the maker reads as a wide apron and raised brayer, the designer as a jagged bob and angled page, the keeper as a high hat and round lenses over a plate folio, and the scout as a lean runner with a trailing scarf and a courier bag. Faces and boots use hard ink edges. Costume colors are shared across the cast. Status keeps its own language through the work tool, open bubble, raised flag and selection ring.
