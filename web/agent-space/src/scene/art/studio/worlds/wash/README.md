# Wash · 雨后水彩

An original watercolor office after the rain. Slate rooftops and river terraces recede into warm paper. Cool light collects at the windows, ochre workbenches mark the work area, and a sage rug and settle mark the lounge. The front threshold, lantern switch and blue postbox with its sleeping cat sit beside a clear route through the office.

| Color | Material and use |
| --- | --- |
| Warm paper `#eee5d2` | Reserved highlights, open walking space and visible paper tooth |
| Ultramarine `#486883` | Window light, slate-blue shadows, apron and mailbox |
| Sage `#799681` | Lounge textiles and wet foliage, carrying less contrast than faces |
| Ochre `#bf9551` and madder `#a86761` | Glazed wood, washed cloth, dispatch seal and switch |

Overlapping translucent shapes give the paint uneven density. Dark edges suggest pooled pigment; broken highlights and small vector deposits show paper tooth and dry brush. A few displacement filters soften large wash boundaries. Faces, tools, feet and status silhouettes use tighter edges so they remain readable at small sizes.

The Maker carries a brush and wears a broad cap and apron. The Designer has swept hair, a madder smock and a swatch page. The Keeper has gray hair, round glasses and a folio. The Scout has a pointed rain hood, a long scarf and a satchel. Roles are costumes; work, availability and attention have separate brush, bubble and pennant glyphs. Selection stays beneath the feet.
