# Terrarium / 苔庭

Original cut-paper SVG art for the Terrarium direction in the [Space worlds design](../../../../../../../../.controller/space-ui-styles/worlds/design.md). The SVGs are the editable masters. The [Studio placement contract](../../../../studio-art.ts) owns intrinsic sizes, contact points and state anchors.

Warm light enters through a curved iron canopy and settles on honey limestone. Forest ink and jade glass establish depth at the rear. Repeated stone joints and iron ribs give the architecture a rhythm at any supported room width. Layered, offset cut edges supply shadow and texture. The entrance tread, workbenches and fern rug form a path from correspondence to work to rest.
