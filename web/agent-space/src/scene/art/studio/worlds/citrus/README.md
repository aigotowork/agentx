# Citrus

柚风小筑 is a bright coastal workroom drawn in mineral white (`#fffdf4`), pale lemon (`#e7c553`), sea-glass aqua (`#83b7aa`) and quiet leaf green (`#71996a`), grounded by sun-warmed oak and small touches of clay. Repeated arch curves, linen weave, fine wood grain and stitched costume marks make the individual props feel like they share one room and one morning light. The hillside-and-bay vista leaves its center open for the office.

The cast shares a 64 × 72 image box and bottom-center foot anchor. A canvas cap and tool apron, copper bob and swatch folio, silver hair and keys, and broad sun hat and chart distinguish the four roles. Signal glyphs retain the Studio meanings; their colors and drawings are decorative and do not represent service state by themselves. Mailbox and cat form one quiet human endpoint.

All 25 editable SVGs are self-contained and use the fixed Studio image IDs and intrinsic dimensions. [proof.html](./proof.html) composes all 25 vectors into one room and repeats the cast at native size. Open it directly in a browser or serve this directory as static files. It is an art composition; Space owns the live placements, status and lamp interaction. The screenshot is retained as [proof.png](./proof.png).
