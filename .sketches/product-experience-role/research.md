## Adoption decision

Reuse shared professional methods, native role/profile contracts and downstream native tests. Add only the focused experience data/relationship agreement and handoff. Do not add a Skill or universal DSL/catalog. UIUX's existing Sitemap, contract and candidate structures provide concrete target identities; a unified actor/need/scenario relation model and cross-view selection remain selected future tool design. Reuse exact TS declarations during any implementation, rather than inferring fields from a screenshot or adding unsupported fields to strict schemas.
