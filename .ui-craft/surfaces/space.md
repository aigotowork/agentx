# Space UI Craft brief

Space is a game-like working office for people directing several long-lived Agents. Their repeated loop is recognize a person → read or compare work → give the next instruction → return to the room. Keep the scene and current work in view during creation and conversation actions. A short toolbar and exact identity take precedence over decorative chrome or standing explanations.

Craft Read: high-density resource workspace, readable long-form content, tool-like precision with a warmer room character. Aim for restrained motion and quiet default operation. Compare a warm compact “field desk” direction and a cooler elegant “reading room” treatment at the same information density; retain the existing office art and canonical style palettes where practical. Reuse the [Space UIUX tokens and components](../../web/agent-space/uiux/README.md); the root UI Craft brief/tokens also contain separate Management decisions. See the [design and tasks](../../.controller/space-canvas-workspace/controller.md) for interaction contracts and evidence.

Learned constraint: assess density by useful message content actually visible on a representative long thread, not by the height of an empty reader. A user should continue reading by scrolling and keep their place across loads or return; explicit retry is for failures. A draft must be comfortable to read and revise as it grows: automatic bounded growth, deliberate resize, and retained history/action access on short screens. Use [the long-conversation experience record](../../.controller/space-canvas-workspace/experience-review.md) to select and exercise realistic paths before accepting changes.
