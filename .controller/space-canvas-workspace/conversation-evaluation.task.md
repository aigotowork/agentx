**Craft Report (Critique, bar 7).** Checked anti-slop, Craft Test, visual hierarchy, clarity, signature and inspiration against real screenshots at 1280×800, 768×1024 and 375×812, plus the 1440×900 two-pane view. The warm office scene is the signature; subdued color, visible titles and a persistent composer support orientation. The repeated filled message containers, generous paragraph/record spacing and two lines of pane chrome spend too much reading height: at 1280×800 Luna shows 2 complete headers and 6 full rendered body lines; at 1440×900 in two panes it shows 3 headers and 9 lines in a 410 px body. More wrapped lines are not inherently more content. An editorial stream of text with hairline separation is the relevant inspiration. Retain scene identity and exact labels; reduce per-record/toolbar overhead, make continuation legible at the fold, and expose a practical editor. Verdict: baseline fails the long read-and-write journey; the scroll retry and guarded unknown outcome are useful behavior to preserve. Dark mode is not offered by the evaluated light artifact; no product edits made.
