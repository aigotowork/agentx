### Operator kill extraction after the output release

`stop_killed` requires an authenticated peer, an existing outer lifecycle transaction, the exact participant incarnation and the current `exit:killed` marker. The lifecycle caller validates the kill ticket, its original peer, generation, PID/incarnation and own marker in that same transaction. Both self-session stop and authorized operator stop call one canonical hard-stop core: enumerate/deduplicate descendants before mutation, freeze all process/terminal effects, retire child-first through existing CAS, preserve initiator and parent_stopped provenance, and propagate every required cleanup/event failure. A nested savepoint prevents a caller that catches a helper error from committing partial descendant retirement. No process I/O occurs there.

CompleteKill owns retained normalized ticket intent plus the entire frozen stop outcome in its outer transaction. The helper deliberately does not create a second retained record under the same request ID. On a lost reply the caller must recover the durable outcome before requiring the deleted live row, then call `validate_stop_outcome` so a namesake successor cannot inherit the old plan. Host cleanup remains independently incarnation-fenced; the outcome does not attest that a signal or cleanup completed.
