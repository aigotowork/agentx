## Prototype structure

Design question: Can a conversation list identify the right conversation quickly while a spatial office answers who works where, with a single exact reader preserving the human's place and work through either entry path?

Considered a world-first view with a floating chat, and a single common directory dressed as two views. The first obscures reading at short heights; the second gives spatial navigation little value. Use a dedicated OfficeMap Block (rooms, desks, activity, directory/keyboard path) and a task-first ConversationIndex Block (message preview, status, exact history and filters), both feeding the same Reader Block. Desktop gives the reader an adjacent column; narrow and short surfaces alternate the source and the same reader inside one viewport. Exact selected object, per-channel scroll/drafts, and per-office map framing belong to the Page, never to either presentation. Asset treatment: original SVG plan and furniture, semantic interactive HTML markers; no production artwork or state.

Use `index.html` for Page/Layout and disclosure shells, `space.css` for local visual/geometry roles on the sibling system foundation, `fixtures.js` for deterministic presentation data, `space.js` for route, page-local interaction and rendered blocks, and `office-plan.svg` for diagram art. A scenario/trigger and evidence register will follow the composed candidate. Coordinate route and scenario vocabulary with [Space experience](space-experience.task.md) and shared controls with [system](design-system.task.md).
