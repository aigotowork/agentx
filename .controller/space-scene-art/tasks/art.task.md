## Art direction and working decision

The target is a small-print workshop at opening time: sun-warmed oak, a rear plaster facade, oil-ink character contours, dusty teal, sumac and saffron accents; stepped cuts and hatching act as print marks. The task is to read the room, distinguish a working participant from an approachable one, and pick out someone who warrants attention. The alternate dark arcade treatment would compete with the reader and make costume and state colors carry too much meaning; the chosen pale ground leaves priority with silhouettes, labels and shape-coded state badges. A single painted room image was rejected because responsive room geometry must remain authoritative. Asset layers and anchor decisions are in the [art contract](../../../web/agent-space/src/scene/art/studio/AGENTS.md). The art proof is a local visual fixture; the integration owns the actual camera, navigable zones, selection and conversation, exact identity and responsive behavior.

The early separate-file interface was sent to vunura (#57686) and kula (#57687). The desk-to-actor foot offset and narrow proof framing are visual assumptions to validate alongside the current geometry. Decorations have no collision or input authority; props should follow supplied fixture geometry.
