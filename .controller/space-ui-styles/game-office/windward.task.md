Own the entire game-quality illustration pack; read [the integration model](design.md) and [Studio dimensions](../../../web/agent-space/src/scene/studio-art.ts). ## Art direction — The lee of the sky

Windward is a bright cliff refuge above a sea of clouds. The eye enters past the dark lee rocks and bent windgrass, follows the breaks in a diagonal cloud bank to a small receiving tower, then returns to pale floorboards and the readable silhouettes of the couriers. Warm open light enters at the upper left; cool air and reflected turquoise shape the shadow planes below and to the right. The center of the vista carries broad, low contrast air so the tiled office and interactive cast own it.

The office is a working observatory assembled by the people who use it: waxed oak at hand height, old turquoise enamel in recesses, beeswax linen in bright patches, brass rubbed bright at hinges and dials. Keep grain in individual short strokes, chip reveals near corners instead of across every surface, and put woven geometry where a hand or foot would find it. The compass and split pennant recur at room, object and badge scales. A uniformly saturated turquoise room or a heavy line around every feature would flatten the scene, so use saturated cool colors in shade and concentrated warm strokes at edges.

At 64 × 72, the maker's round flight cap and leather apron, designer's wide survey hat and plum cloak, keeper's silver bun and indigo supply coat, and scout's peaked forelock and saffron scarf distinguish jobs through silhouette before details. The cast share a breeze blowing toward the right. Idle retains visible hands and an open stance; walking lends itself to a light vertical compression and directional turn; working/settled can lower the torso while preserving the face, feet and hand prop. Motion, signals and lamp state belong to the runtime.
