# Governance content authoring

Use the [harness-authoring domain](domains/harness-authoring/AGENTS.md) when creating or maintaining principles, Roles, Scenarios, Skills, entry points or supporting content. Select its routes for scenario design, writing, external adaptation, composition and validation; reuse guidance already read.

For new content or a material change to its situation, method or boundaries, begin with [scenario design](domains/harness-authoring/scenarios/scenario-design.md). Record the scope, rationale, method and effectiveness, conditions and limits in the owner's relevant sketch or design. Examine both the user's intended outcome and the Agent's actual visible context, including misleading or ineffective instructions and unnecessary burden. A bounded wording correction can reuse still-valid design reasoning.

Keep author rationale in those records and actionable information in the consuming guide. General document expression and placement remain in [documentation](documentation.md); metadata contracts remain in [shared structures](refs/xschema/common-schema.md). The domain owns detailed authoring methods; this file is their shared entry.
