# Shared scenarios

Keep general, cross-domain and actionable scenarios without a settled domain here. State the trigger, useful inputs, method and expected outcome; connect the entry route in the parent AGENTS.md. A few files need no extra taxonomy.

Group a stable subject family into a domain when that improves discovery. Move its references with it and repair incoming links; do not retain two editable copies. Unsettled ideas and experiments belong in the owning `.sketches/` until usable conclusions are promoted.

Scopes may contain their own roles, scenarios, skills, refs and scripts as needed. Project-specific methods belong in the consuming project's `.agent-guides/local/`, whose entry selects the relevant content.
