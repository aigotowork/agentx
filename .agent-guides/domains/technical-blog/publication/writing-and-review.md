# Write and review the explanation

Use for drafting, substantial editing, translation and editorial review. Select the [primary form](../planning/expression-design.md) before a prose outline. Review the complete experience, including the runnable work or video when it carries the argument. General expression conventions remain in [documentation](../../../documentation.md); delivery checks remain in [renderer and release](renderer-and-release.md).

## Make the causal chain readable

Start with the consequential question, observable failure or task in the selected medium. Establish the recognizable situation and what the reader will be able to do. A controlled example or brief demonstration can precede extended prose. Generic claims about changing technology add no necessary premise.

| Kind of explanation | Useful progression |
| --- | --- |
| Mechanism | Trigger → necessary model → operation → failure boundary → usable rule |
| Comparison | Shared constraints → plausible alternatives → decisive cases → costs → selection conditions |
| Investigation | Symptom → competing hypotheses → discriminating evidence → cause → repair and limits |
| Migration | Before/after contracts → compatibility states → rollout order → failure/recovery → completion |
| Experiment | Prediction/question → manipulable model → observation → cause → changed real-system case |

These are causal choices, not mandatory headings. A storyboard or interaction sequence can carry the same relationships. Explain what changes which outcome: a guard rejects a request, a write order preserves a fact or a particular case distinguishes a failure class. Lists of technologies do not provide that explanation.

Introduce important concepts before relying on them. Distinguish a teaser from the first inference that requires the definition. Keep names, units, conditions and direction consistent across text, diagrams, code, controls, narration and captions. Use the content's intended language; preserve standard identifiers and supply another-language term where it adds precision.

Show the smallest code slice that reveals the mechanism, including consequential guards and failure behavior. Label pseudocode. Link maintained native source for full implementation and explain a meaningful input/output or trace. Avoid line-by-line syntax narration and copied files that drift.

Give each figure or experiment a local question, reading instruction, observation and consequence. Put definitions near their use. Optional derivations, full traces and secondary cases can use local disclosure when supported; the central reasoning must work without opening everything. Finish with a transferable decision, boundary or diagnostic and its remaining trade-off.

## Review the promise before polish

First reconstruct the argument in your own words: the reader's problem, central claim, supporting basis, inference, conditions and meaningful challenge. Its support can be in controls, traces, figures or narration. If reconstruction fails, distinguish missing explanation from your own missing domain prerequisites before judging the artifact.

| Concern | Passing question | Repair |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reader outcome | Is the specific decision/action clear and usable elsewhere? | Narrow/reframe or route exact usage to Docs |
| Argument | Does each substantial part add to the central question? | Remove, reconnect or split the digression |
| Model | Are objects, states, identities, responsibilities and invariants coherent? | Repair meaning before expression |
| Evidence | Do important claims have appropriate current basis? | Research, qualify or remove |
| Challenge | Is there a credible alternative and a distinguishing failure/boundary case? | Challenge the thesis or narrow it |
| Form | Is the decisive relationship observable at justified reader effort? | Revisit form or sequence before adding media/prose |
| Materials | Do framing, edits, holds, annotations and audio serve their roles in context? | Repair the affected scene or channel version |
| Views | Do figures answer useful questions without implying unsupported guarantees? | Correct scope, relation or omitted path |
| Experiment/work | Can readers operate, reproduce and interpret the contrast? | Repair rules/feedback or choose a static route |
| Prerequisites | Are terms, units, assumptions and omitted detail visible when needed? | Define, relocate or simplify |
| Delivery | Do the selected source, renderer, playback and interactions actually work? | Fix the artifact and retain draft status until required checks pass |
| Maintenance | Are source basis, owner, correction path and refresh triggers discoverable? | Record the missing relationships |

Material blockers include fabricated results/citations, false guarantees, a figure contradicting the mechanism, unavailable essential interaction, essential content lost without its medium, private-data exposure or an inaccessible essential control. Minor polish can remain follow-up work once the promise is met. Scores, visual polish, length, media count and a large bibliography cannot compensate for a broken central claim.

For a substantive finding, record location → observed defect → likely reader misunderstanding → correction → evidence that resolves it. Distinguish a confirmed defect, reasonable alternative, unknown and style preference. Revise facts/model first, then reasoning and experience, then local expression. Recheck only affected claims and views after correction.

For formulas, check variables, units, value domains, time basis, assumptions, boundary values and at least one worked calculation where it clarifies the relation. Symbolic exactness is not measurement precision. An inequality is meaningful only after defining which real or teaching objects it counts; formulas are unnecessary when a plain statement is more precise.

## Preserve meaning when editing or translating

Diagnose a concrete ambiguity, repetition, missing bridge or inappropriate register. Make a local correction when adequate. Preserve actors, independent facts, time, conditions, negation, attribution and certainty. “May help only with caching enabled; unverified and planned” cannot become “faster and released.” Do not invent personal experience, emotions, incidents or numbers to make prose sound natural.

Keep precise technical terms stable when variety would blur identity. Punctuation, sentence length and word frequency are contextual clues rather than quality verdicts. Preserve code, links, metadata, identifiers and published anchors unless the actual change requires them. A semantic edit preserving a claim does not independently verify that claim.

Translate real content with equivalent claims, figures, controls, captions and uncertainty. Do not create empty/copied pages to claim language coverage. Apply the consumer's actual locale and component UI rules; no universal catalog mechanism is assumed here.

## Use reader tasks for unresolved explanation risks

When evidence about comprehension is needed and appropriate readers are available, give them a meaningful task: predict a changed schedule, identify a failure boundary, explain a rule or apply it to a new case. Record the content version, reader prerequisites, task, observed answer/difficulty and the corresponding revision. A small formative observation can locate defects; it does not prove statistical significance or general learning improvement.

Separate reaction, learning, actual work behavior and business outcome. Enjoyment, completion and time on page can have several explanations. Before/after comparisons need comparable tasks and account for prior knowledge and practice effects. Ordinary editing does not require a formal study, and an unrun task design remains proposed evidence.

## Publish and refresh honestly

Use a title and description matching the actual question. Preserve real editorial identity and accurate publication dates; do not backdate or invent operational history. Link related material by its conceptual role and exact usage documentation where appropriate.

Preserve published identity, URLs and meaningful anchors. Use the consumer's alias/redirect mechanism when changing them. Record invalidating changes such as runtime upgrades, contract/policy changes, persistence predicates, benchmark setup or teaching rules. Recheck the affected claim and dependent views rather than the entire publication by default.

Correct substantive errors visibly. Keep original measurements identifiable and label later observations rather than silently rewriting history. Use actual reader questions to refine content and method, keeping effectiveness claims within the evidence collected.
