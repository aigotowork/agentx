# Logo and visual identity

Use for designing or reviewing logos, wordmarks, monograms, emblems, identifying characters and visual identity families; for reference/style work that supports them; and for explicitly requested logo collaboration tools. A spelling correction or existing icon export follows its owning asset workflow. A request for a method or architecture authorizes that deliverable, not artwork production or tool implementation.

Read [foundations](foundations.md) when starting or changing a direction. Select all matching routes below, reuse established decisions and enter at the current design question. A bounded kerning correction needs the accepted source, relevant construction and delivery checks, not a fresh brand workshop.

- Begin with brand situation and intended expression. An industry label does not select an aesthetic. Ornamental, figurative, typographic, material and restrained work remain eligible.
- Distinguish semantic concept, subject, visual language, construction, rendering and use. Recoloring or refinishing one outline normally produces variants of a direction.
- Extract relationships from attributed references. Generated studies, external references, editable identity sources and mockups have different roles.
- Define a family only for actual uses. A rich primary emblem may need a compact mark; the small-use constraint does not dictate the primary aesthetic.
- Carry user decisions, exact target versions and inspection restrictions across rounds. Preserve the user's statement separately from the Agent's interpretation. Do not impose questionnaires, fixed candidate counts or recurring approval gates.
- Deliver what exists and state what was checked. A valid SVG, construction grid, successful operation or preference does not establish recognition, legal availability or creative quality.

The domain is usable with the consumer's existing records and available asset tools. It supplies no image library, renderer, geometry solver, editor connector, background service or running workbench. The vocabularies are extensible seed data, not schemas, quality scores or registered UI capabilities. [Sources](research/sources.md) preserves attribution and historical access limits; reading the author's research checkout is never a prerequisite.
