# Sources and adoption limits

This domain adapts the longwork xsaas project's Logo and visual identity method, surveyed for Harness on 2026-11-13. Its reference-role model, concept signatures, identity-family method, collaborative rounds, source/version records and open vocabularies are the source authors' synthesis. Harness retains that professional breadth and rewrites project bindings into consumer-owned source/tool discovery. Neither the author checkout nor its research cache is needed to use the method.

This is a reasoned operating method, not a certification standard, complete design history or empirical guarantee. Attribution does not imply endorsement. No third-party Skill bundle, script, PDF/card deck, font, example artwork or reference image is redistributed with it. The guides explain mechanisms in their own terms; the two curated YAML vocabularies are method resources. Rights for material actually incorporated into a design still depend on that material and use.

## Synthesis, delivery and evidence boundaries

The concept signature, reference roles, morphological matrix, facet vocabulary, style recipes, source/variant/presentation records, coverage checks and two-loop collaboration model are authored synthesis. The workbench's rounds/input snapshots, correctable interpretations, save distinctions and recovery requirements are proposed design decisions; no implemented workspace or observed user study is claimed.

The portable edition retains craft and collaboration, carries complete vocabularies, groups content by responsibility and replaces production paths with the consuming asset owner's contract. Unbuilt services, proposed endpoints, author cache paths and specific production generators are not prerequisites. [Records](../collaboration/records.md) defines semantic meanings; an actual implementation owns its exact native schema and operational behavior.

Static walkthroughs can show coherent choices and reachable context. Link/metadata/resource checks establish only their structural properties. Actual creative quality, audience recognition, fabrication, runtime recovery and improved user effort require relevant observed work. The [evaluation method](../evaluation.md) keeps these evidence types separate.

When updating, add a source only if it changes a decision or exposes a boundary. Record actual reading depth, source/version, adoption choice and limits. Recheck any dependency that the consumer actually needs. Do not silently activate downloaded instructions, copy material because a cache exists, or generalize a tool-route finding to an entire application.
