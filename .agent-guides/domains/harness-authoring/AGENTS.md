# Harness authoring

Use this domain when designing or maintaining principles, Roles, Scenarios, Skills, entry points or their supporting content. It owns the authoring method; the subject domain owns professional decisions, and the project owns its runtime authority and publication process.

Select all matching routes. Reuse context already read. A wording-only correction can reuse the existing design; it does not require a new design exercise.

| Situation | Read/apply | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New guidance or a material change to its situation, method, workflow or boundaries | [Scenario design](scenarios/scenario-design.md) | An owned sketch/design explaining the situation, rationale, effectiveness and user/Agent fit |
| Create or revise a principle, Role, Scenario or Skill | [Authoring](scenarios/authoring.md), then [writing](writing.md) for changed expression | A usable method with one owner and appropriate scope |
| Extract a method from a report, repository, Skill or work evidence | [Adaptation](scenarios/adaptation.md) | A compatible method with traceable adoption choices |
| Connect scenarios, change an entry or reorganize guidance/resources | [Composition](scenarios/composition.md) | Explicit reading conditions, relationships and ownership |
| Check a draft or a change to behavior, routing or required resources | [Validation](scenarios/validation.md) | Focused evidence and clearly bounded claims |

General document expression and placement stay in [documentation](../../documentation.md); metadata stays in [shared structures](../../refs/xschema/common-schema.md). Follow the relevant sections when needed. These references do not require loading every linked method or authorize tools or delegation.

New or materially changed guidance must have scenario reasoning in the owner's relevant sketch or design. Keep author rationale there; keep information needed to act in the consuming guide. No fixed headings, second task tracker or separate reviewer is required. Source editing and installation follow the consuming project's actual maintenance entry.
