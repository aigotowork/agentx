# Adapt an external method

Use when taking a workflow, rule or capability from a report, repository, Skill or work evidence into the current system. Establish the target [situation and design basis](scenario-design.md); reading a source does not activate its modes, authorize its scripts or adopt all its rules.

## Extract the mechanism

Identify the source/version and relevant scope. Separate observed facts, author's claims, proposed rules, examples and measured results. Extract what matters: triggering situation, assumptions, decisions, actions, outputs, limits and why the method is expected to help. A popular source or a short prompt is not evidence that it fits this project.

Compare with current authoritative methods before adding content. Choose reference only, unchanged adoption, narrow adaptation, a rewritten method or no adoption. Record the material choice and its rationale in the owning sketch/design; retain source and attribution requirements when redistributing content.

## Fit the method to the system

| Concern | Resolve before treating the result as usable guidance |
| --- | --- |
| Meaning and ownership | Map concepts and responsibilities to current owners; reuse an equivalent method rather than introducing parallel terminology |
| Authority and interaction | Remove conflicts with actual permissions and explicit user requirements; do not import mandatory delegation, repeated approval or persistent modes by implication |
| State and artifacts | Map logs, ledgers, outputs and decisions to existing destinations; avoid a second editable state tracker |
| Discovery and references | Repair source-only paths and undeclared related capabilities; ensure the consumer can reach what is required |
| Tools and environment | Identify commands, versions, resources and platform assumptions; define honest behavior when a prerequisite is unavailable |
| Effectiveness and limits | Preserve important exceptions and verify the target cases; do not generalize results beyond their evidence |

Borrow expression separately from policy. A coding Skill's decision ladder can inspire document authoring without importing “fewest files”, omitted error handling or a code-first response format. A report's recommendation still needs a scope and an action before it becomes an instruction.

## Integrate and validate

Publish one coherent method in its owning location, with necessary source references. Keep long comparison notes in author records. Carry required references with the capability; do not make consumers depend on an author's research checkout. Refer to authoritative tool and distribution contracts for executable dependencies.

Walk through a representative target case and the boundary most likely to fail. Check applicable [user and Agent perspectives](validation.md), including whether compact wording now obscures a condition or whether an upstream instruction overrides local responsibilities.

Leave with an adoption decision, compatible guidance and explicit remaining limits. If the source adds no useful capability, recording why it was not adopted is a complete result. Future updates revisit changed source assumptions and local adaptations; they do not blindly replace the local method.
