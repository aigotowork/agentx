# Unified office management

The user authorized all assessed office capabilities together on 2026-11-15 and preferred one holistic design/implementation over separately delivered A/B/C stages. Adopt [the unified model and contracts](../agent-management/offices.md), building on the [source-verified assessment](../agent-management/office-supply-assessment.md). Internal dependencies remain necessary, but no separate release/approval gate divides adopted membership, office/post browsing, pure route inspection and Role/directory actions.

The existing native storage, endpoint arbitration, launch runtime and management operation receipts remain authoritative. Bind the existing embedded management console to these capabilities and publish native-generated reusable supply for the visual office consumer. Preserve the independent offline visual-office frontend/LAN preview and the earlier accepted r4 artifact. Full-disk inventory, a new office registry, unified Skills/model telemetry and live rollout are not required for this outcome. [Delivery](../../.controller/office-management/delivery.md) owns actual execution and evidence.
