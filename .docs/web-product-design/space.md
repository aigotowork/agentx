# Space: find the conversation and keep your place

Space helps a human figure out who to talk to, recover what happened, and continue with the right Agent. This is the target experience for the synthetic preview; the [native concept model](../agent-space/concept-alignment.md), [current routes](../agent-space/routes.md) and [Space module](../../web/agent-space/AGENTS.md) retain authority over production facts.

## First usable path contract

Use one scoped workspace with **Conversations** and **Office** presentations, a shared Agent conversation, and **Mailbox** as a scope-wide human inbox. Conversations is the efficient way to find a known Agent and resume work; Office makes office membership, work/rest placement and available people legible at a glance. Offer both modes persistently. Use Conversations as the initial entry for the new preview; honor explicit mode in a link. Keep the production route default unchanged when first binding the design. Office switch, search and status are shared discovery state. A mode switch preserves an open conversation, channel, read position and draft; scene selection highlights/reveals its exact anchor without changing zoom. A filtered, off-presentation or stopped reference retains its reader and uses a truthful cue; do not fabricate a scene avatar.

Desktop: workspace chrome → office/scope picker and mode → list or scene beside one reader. Narrow: same shell, workspace and reader take turns in the body. **Back to list/office** returns to the triggering exact row/actor (or a stable discovery control if gone), preserving query, list position and camera. While reading on narrow screens, switching mode changes the return destination and keeps reading. Selecting another office is a deliberate discovery pivot: close the reader, keep its draft, restore destination office camera and clear incompatible resources. A specific cross-office navigation (relation/mail/URL) opens the exact subject in its actual office. Do not open a namesake by fallback.

The common conversation header shows subject, office and current/stopped identity cue, with **Share**, **Details** and context actions behind local controls. **Messages** is the default AgentX envelope channel; **Provider records** is a separate explicitly loaded channel, with attributed expandable tool/reasoning when actually supplied. Local notes may be a third channel; they never become messages. Recent messages open at latest; **Load older** prepends without losing reading position; new messages away from the end show a jump-to-latest cue. Composer belongs to Messages only; a stopped subject is read-only. Leave entered text/attachments in a page-owned exact-reference draft until accepted or deliberately discarded.

For the core prototype seed a canvas with at least two populated offices, an empty office, clustered agents sharing a Role, a current and stopped namesake, and a current target reachable from list and canvas. Give that target existing AgentX messages, an older page and Provider records suitable for disclosure. Capture exact references with synthetic scope/generation/kind/native-ID equivalents. Visible fixtures should allow Accepted, Rejected and Outcome unknown; show scenario controls as demo-only. Accepted adds one outgoing envelope and clears the submitted draft. Rejected keeps it editable. Unknown keeps the draft and blocks repetition against that reference; **Check outcome** is read-only and reconciles only the original attempt. Do not simulate a reply or infer execution from acceptance.

Core acceptance IDs: **SP-01** known target → read → edit draft → switch modes → return/reopen exact subject; **SP-02** browse/reveal/crowded scene → focus exact actor → reset/return without zoom jump; **SP-03** current vs stopped → share/reopen exact → no retarget; **SP-04** latest/older + explicit Provider load/disclosure → restore Messages position and draft; **SP-05** deliberate send acceptance/rejection/unknown → preserve or reconcile only original data; **SP-06** narrow iframe → find/read/compose/disclose/return using same App.

## Goals and evidence

The [Delivery](../../.controller/web-product-design/delivery.delivery.md) asks for a coherent conversation workspace with useful list and spatial paths. The [current App](../../web/agent-space/src/App.tsx) already has one controller, two presentations and per-office camera memory. Its shared controls and module evidence support preservation of that architecture, but do not establish which mode users prefer. The table describes task situations, not interviewed personas or permissions. The [baseline walkthrough](../../.controller/web-product-design/evidence/baseline/README.md) checked exact current selection and draft retention through mode changes and browser Back at 1440×900 and 390×844. Preserve those working properties. At 390px the baseline scene reader offers both a window close and conversation back; the target uses one clear contextual return for either mode, with a distinct browser Back history. Compare matched current-reader and discovery states at those widths; fixtures differ, so do not compare row counts or infer a time or preference improvement. The baseline demo only composes local notes, so no baseline live-send comparison is possible. See the [baseline inventory](baseline.md#space-locate-converse-disclose-return) for current disclosures and friction.

| Situation | Question and success | Design hypothesis to test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Returning collaborator, remembers a name or work description | “Where were we?” Locate the exact live subject, scan latest or older messages, continue without losing a draft. | Text search + current/by-office grouping costs less effort than finding a known actor on the canvas. An honest draft cue is useful even when there is no conversation index metadata. |
| Human entering an unfamiliar or busy office | “Who is here and where can I start?” Read offices/zones, inspect candidates, open the intended exact subject. | Spatial snapshot plus a room directory helps exploration. Status, office and responsibility inform contact; position, animation and activity never imply readiness, progress or acceptance. |
| Reader following a handoff, link or inbound letter | “Is this the exact person or record the sender meant?” Resolve the shared reference, inspect sender/source and return without losing the previous context. | Current/stopped and office cues are sufficient for short-list comparison; detailed native identity is one disclosure away. |
| Human interrupted by an error, a resize or host context capture | “Is there still work to do, and can I safely continue?” Locate send state or return to the retained draft. | A local state cue and original-receipt lookup prevent resubmission better than a generic error banner. Same responsive App keeps the return path available. |

Initial design choices: prefer the compact list for re-entry; do not build two chat stacks or use an overlay attached to a moving actor as the only reader. Use spatial cues for orientation rather than a workflow diagram or decorative status chart. No assumed cross-Agent unread count, recency ordering, reply preview or last-seen time: the current Space consumer reads an exact subject's records when selected and the scope-human mailbox has its own genuine unhandled ledger. Prototype may seed preview snippets if marked as synthetic; production list works without them. Product validation can later compare time to correct target and wrong-instance opens for known-name versus unfamiliar-office tasks, plus whether people can explain Unknown without retrying. Independent walkthrough is evidence of operability only.

## Canonical workspace and navigation

| Page or region | Entry and primary action | Disclosures, state and exit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Space shell | Persistent office chooser (all, exact directory, confirmed Global, unknown and legacy when supplied), Conversations/Office mode, Mailbox entry. Search and textual status filter in discovery. | Connection/coverage/operations summary opens scope facts and original receipts. Workspace overflow exposes Posts, Skills (when unavailable, say so), Contact directory, and New Agent as appropriate. Search matches supplied name, responsibility/Role and office; result counts label loaded coverage, with more/loading/error where applicable. Scope is visible without opening details. |
| Conversations index | Current by default; group by adopted office in all-offices view. Row: name, responsibility if supplied, runtime label, draft cue; distinguish stopped with explicit record kind. Search/status within selected office and Current or Stopped independently. | Stopped is an explicit directory section, not a filter on a live person's work status. No matches offers clear filters; true empty office offers change office and available Posts. Partial results offer load more; failed reads do not imply empty. Keep a selected reader when a shared filter hides its row; label the cause, allow reset. |
| Office canvas | Room labels, stable seats/anchors, current people and known empty offices. All-offices panorama can enter a room without treating parent directories as roll-ups; focused office shows useful layout/adjacency at working zoom. | Pan, zoom in/out and Reset view work by buttons as well as pointer; explicit pan/zoom do not select or submit. Expose room/actor directory alongside map for crowded or overlapping anchors and as full keyboard path; selection from directory matches actor identity and panning. Stopped opens via archive/directory list with no actor. |
| Shared conversation | Exact selection from index, actor, URL, post member, Mailbox sender or launch relation. Read the channel, share the address, continue work. | Details, Work and Relations, target actions, provenance and individual record detail stay local. Desktop can keep discovery beside reader; narrow replaces discovery with reader and a contextual Back. Return restores selected row/actor or stable control when absent. |
| Mailbox | Scope-wide unhandled count; **Pending** and **History** with bounded pages; exact sender can open their conversation. | Expand a letter, reveal provenance, mark explicit letter or displayed-page IDs handled; reading never handles. Legacy name-only sender stays readable but unlinked. Return retains mailbox cursor/tab then prior discovery or reader. |
| Posts and Skills | Office overflow → Posts list or native-supported Skills. Post detail shows current definition and actual location/origin; open selected exact adopted member or deliberately launch per supported contract. | Post dialog uses current resolved source and native fields. Adoption uses stored definition source, not current short-name equality. Skills unsupported is distinct from empty. Return to same office/mode and prior discovery filters. |
| Contact directory | Office overflow → input real directory; **Preview route** first, then contact the resolved entry only when eligible. | Present actual office/Role/endpoint. Editing the directory immediately invalidates the displayed route and disables its actions until a fresh preview. Ambiguous, stale, unavailable and missing routes explain blocked send. A display name or current room never supplies target authority. |
| Local actions | Reader/actor menu shows existing native eligible actions; New Agent lives in workspace overflow. | Targeted Resume, Fork, Stop participation and Terminate process keep their separate meanings. Consequential controls confirm actual subject/action first; report receipt, do not swap subject on resume/fork. Escape closes top disclosure only and restores its trigger. |

Office changes clear incompatible post/mail cursor state and close the reader unless entering by exact target; do not mutate the drafts map. Keep one return locus per navigation source, including office, discovery mode, list/tab/filter/scroll/camera and initiating control. Back in the reader returns locally; browser Back/Forward restores compatible addressable state. Cross-office launches/relationships/mail sender entry may change discovery office to actual native membership without rewriting history silently; return goes to the originating disclosure with its selection/page. Local modal and tool expansion do not create history entries. URL includes existing office/view/tab/exact record/channel/filter/post/panel/mail/facts parameters where applicable, with legacy hints/hash preserved; copy only location, never a draft, attachment bytes, receipt ID or private action options. On restoration, exact-resolve once per intent with bounds; loading, stale and not found states keep explicit feedback and a safe way back. Never send, handle, upload or acquire Provider records from a URL.

### Canvas behavior that earns its space

At overview fit, reserve readable room labels and population/completeness. Prefer an explicit cluster or room count over overlapping tiny labels; open a bounded named directory at dense zoom. At working zoom, show non-overlapping name anchors when possible and preserve positions across filtering; hide or mute unmatched people without moving per-person seats. Selection from actor or directory focuses the same exact person; pan only as needed to reveal it, preserve zoom, and keep an independent visual and accessible selected cue. Manual pan/zoom is opt-in, camera is per office, Reset fits the current office or panorama, and return after reading restores the camera from before reveal (explicit user camera edits are retained). A floor with no agents is a valid office. Add room directory affordance and stable scroll for keyboard/nonspatial use; do not require dragging, scrolling, hover or differentiating colors. Offer pause/reduced motion with no loss of status cues; animation cannot be an interaction precondition. Native workspace, motion and human mailbox truth remain anchored in the [scene ownership guide](../../web/agent-space/src/scene/AGENTS.md).

## One conversation across presentations

| Region | Information and decision | Disclosure / transition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reader heading | Short name, office breadcrumb, current/stopped badge and text status for exact target. If state goes stale, retain identified reader and last-read context with a stale label, gate mutation. | Share and Details at the heading; show full exact reference/copy, adopted Role source vs cwd, launch title, Profile/tool/model only when supplied. Links to related exact records read-only and preserve return. Historical target remains read-only for sending; resuming is a separate action with separate result. |
| Channels | **AgentX Messages** vs **Provider records** vs secondary **Local notes**. The selected channel never changes the authority or target of the reader. | Messages show sender, recipient context, time and body; thread/reply/intent and provenance expand. Provider starts with **Load Provider records** (only if supported); show loading, empty, not supplied and read failure separately. Display only actually supplied material; role, exchange and tool/reasoning details expand in place, rich blocks synthetic only until native support. Notes are page-local, separately drafted and saved; no outbound effect. |
| Reading | Start at most recent visible message in target design. While scrollable, older pagination preserves the top visible record and its offset; channel/mode switches restore the per-channel anchor. Where the full page fits or the browser clamps the offset at the end, keep the original record visible and restore reading focus/context there instead of claiming equal geometry. | Label beginning-of-history only when known, otherwise use “Older history unavailable”/partial. When new records arrive while reading older messages, show **New messages · Jump to latest**; don't move focus or scroll. Safe Markdown; plain links require a deliberate click, inert remote images, deliberate registered attachments/copy. |
| Composer | Messages only, with explicit Send, target visible, safe newline editing and attachments/options behind controls. Input stays bounded so the feed and Send remain reachable. | Attach/upload failure retains text and already accepted references; options (intent, thread, reply) are part of the exact draft target design. Leave the old draft on selection changes. Preserve draft on cancel, rejection, unconfirmed/Unknown or stale. IME composition never sends. Do not copy private draft payload into URL or cross-target context. |
| Work and relations | File-bound Task/Delivery health and direct launch origin/children belong to compact contextual disclosures, separate from record feed. | Show declared work state, observed binding/diagnostic and source separately from runtime. Only use direct native relation facts; unknown/unretained stays unavailable without guessing. Cross-link exact subjects and offer return. |

Responsive target: one responsive working-region boundary; at narrow widths switch from side-by-side discovery+reader to one body surface, keep mode/office controls and a reachable composer. Keep headers small, put long paths/IDs/errors behind a named control while exposing short actionable state, allow the history and bounded secondary disclosures to scroll without moving the whole shell. At 390×844 iframe and at 320px short height the heading, Back and Send stay reachable with long content. Use actual buttons, selectable inputs and meaningful statuses, visible focus, sensible reading order and contextual Escape. Long message URLs/code and body scroll must stay within the reader. Host captures remain explicit, exact-revision guarded and never send; mode changes alone are presentation, while office/subject changes invalidate capture-to-commit per [responsive bridge](../agent-space/responsive-space.md#minimal-selected-context-bridge-for-wrapper-owners).

### Operation result and recovery

| State | UI result | Next allowed action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Available | Valid exact current target, can-send, no unresolved operation; non-empty supported draft. | Explicit Send captures target, request and submitted draft revision; registered attachments keep their IDs. |
| Submitting / awaiting | Show Sending and block duplicate dispatch; retain original payload/ID. No optimistic Agent reply or false accepted bubble. | Leave this view or check status when supported; other exact drafts remain isolated. |
| Accepted | “Message accepted” (or precise reported stage); show one outgoing envelope when supplied by native records, including each accepted attachment reference once; preview simulates the ledger. | Clear only the submitted revision/components, keeping any later edits. Delivery to the Agent, execution and reply are independent facts. |
| Rejected/no effect confirmed | Show bounded reason, retain draft; known definite rejection is distinct from missing result. | Correct and explicitly send once allowed by native eligibility and no-effect guarantee. |
| Unconfirmed / Unknown | “Send outcome unknown · Draft kept”; hold repeated sends, include scope/target cue and original receipt action. Never label a failed lookup as rejection. | Check original receipt by GET only. Reconcile a definite result, if any; pending/missing/terminal Unknown may remain blocked. No invented retry/resubmit. Preview should include a recoverable dropped response case and a persistent unknown case if available. |
| Not eligible / stale identity | Read-only or unavailable with reason such as stopped, scope changed, lookup failed, capability lost. | Restore exact target if available, select a different target deliberately, or leave; do not transfer draft by name or treat a successor as continuation. |

Accepted refers to durable AgentX acceptance where the receipt supports it, never automatic proof that a Provider saw the message. Existing [runtime diagnostics](../architecture/runtime-diagnostics.md) and [operation journal/controller](../../web/agent-space/src/live/controller.ts) own recovery and public contract limits. Target preview outcome controls are clearly synthetic and are outside normal production Send affordances.

## Scenarios and observable criteria

Use a fresh synthetic context per independent path. Keep one fixed known current subject across list and actor, a different stopped record under the same name, two nonempty offices, an empty office, and a dense room. Include long text, an older page, a Provider exchange and nested detail, accepted/rejected/unknown send, and useful partial/empty/error states. Use exact fixture IDs in run records. Preview product success here is distinct from usability, actual native operation, and user acceptance.

## Production parity and engineering sequence

| Carry forward | Current source authority | Target change / condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Office discovery, current/stopped, exact read/selection, known/unknown/legacy/global, route history and bridge | [Space guide](../../web/agent-space/AGENTS.md), [routes](../agent-space/routes.md), [responsive bridge](../agent-space/responsive-space.md) | First migrate presentation/return state using existing controller and URL parser; test old deep links, embedded hints and exact late resolution. Keep current production route defaults and legacy panel/tab addresses. Separate selected record from focused layout; preserve camera/cache boundaries. |
| Scene composition/cat/room size/bounded presence | [scene runtime](../../web/agent-space/src/scene/AGENTS.md), [office details](../../.controller/office-details/design.md) | Improve directory affordances and fit without removing deliberate mailbox/scene interactions, reduced motion, sparse and dense room behavior. Preserve inspection tooling as contextual developer access; no new renderer or inspection design needed. |
| Message and Provider reading | [read model](../../.controller/live-ui-integration/tasks/space.md#scenario--supply--gaps-2026-11-15), [controller](../../web/agent-space/src/live/controller.ts), [reader](../../web/agent-space/src/conversation/Conversation.tsx) | The [native query](../../src/management/store.rs) and controller expose only a bounded oldest-first `after` cursor, defaulting to the start. The target latest/older path needs a qualified native latest-window and backward cursor before it can ship without reading unbounded history. Do not simulate end-of-history completeness by draining the current cursor. Preserve source attribution and explicit receipt-backed Provider acquisition. Expose richer Provider blocks only when reliable structured supply exists. |
| Compose, accepted/uncertain recovery, upload/context and local notes | [operations and receipts](../../web/agent-space/src/live/controller.ts), [context contract](../agent-management/web-context.md) | Reuse exact receipt journal and GET-only recovery; improve placement and preserve submitted revision. Target draft options/per-channel anchors need frontend ownership; drafts remain page-owned, no cross-tab persistence implied. Keep existing limits/options and host capture revision fences. |
| Mailbox, Posts, Skills, directory preview/contact, new Agent and lifecycle | [current actions](../../web/agent-space/src/live/Actions.tsx), [Space guide](../../web/agent-space/AGENTS.md) | Move to contextual disclosures first; production parity gate covers current eligible operations before any old surface is removed. Secondary effects may be deferred in preview only. Do not invent a Skills catalog. Preserve explicit mailbox letter set and native pagination/eligibility. |
| Files and launch relations | [work binding source](../../web/agent-space/src/conversation/BoundWork.tsx), [launch relations](../../.controller/agent-launch-relations/design.md) | Put these behind compact reader disclosures; retain native paging, stale/absence semantics and exact cross-office identity. No global topology/history or synthetic inferred work facts in production. |

Sequence by risk: (1) exact shared reader/list/canvas transitions and routes without changing durable operations; (2) reader history/composer and operation feedback with native read and fault cases; (3) resource disclosure and current-capability parity; (4) optional recency/unread or richer Provider display only with evidenced need and qualified supply. Legacy route handling and same-app responsiveness are release gates at each migrated slice. Each future native integration needs own disposable verification before any live adoption.

Open questions for Controller: whether list should become the production default after a product decision; whether richer conversation indexing is worth new supply; what native latest-window/backward read contract is feasible for bounded histories. Owners: product/Controller for default and discovery demand, native/SDK for durable history and indexing, prototype/evaluation for disclosed simulation scope. Continue with the stable current contract without waiting on those decisions.
