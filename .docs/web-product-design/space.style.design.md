# Space: working room

[Foundation and public contract](system.md) · [Specimen](../../.controller/web-product-design/preview/system/)

People are finding a collaborator and returning to a thread. Warm paper and ink frame the scene, with a leaf-green accent for current selection and deliberate action. Compared with Management, the reader and actions get more breathing room, softer corners and a slightly larger body size. Fine dividers organize chat chronology; do not decorate each message as a card. Use system line icons in controls and reserve pixel art, silhouettes, weather and scene motion for the product-owned office layer. Keep scene detail out of the conversation’s reading surface.

List and office are two complementary ways to reach one conversation. An overlay can explain a person or route, but broader context can live beside the reader where it does not intercept composing. A narrow layout makes return explicit and preserves page-owned drafts. The same named tones carry uncertainty and failure irrespective of the art. Dark is an independent UI surface mapping in this specimen; pixel-art adaptation and dark coverage of the full office are product decisions. Keep scene movement optional or reducible and avoid animations on routine switch, send, or dismissal.
